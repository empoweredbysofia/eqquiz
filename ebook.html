This is not a self-help book.

It does not tell you to think positive. It does not ask you to be grateful for the hard things. It does not promise that everything happens for a reason.

What it does is give you the language, the frameworks, and the practical tools to understand what your emotions are actually doing — and what to do next.

It is written for women who are tired of feeling like their emotions are a problem to be managed. Who have tried the breathing exercises, the journaling, the gratitude lists. Who know something deeper is needed but have not had the words for it until now.
