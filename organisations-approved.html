The EmpowerED Teacher
Because the teacher in the room changes everything
CPD certified
Most suitable for: All schools · Secondary focus · MATs · NQTs · PGCE programmes · College & FE
✦ View the session guide →

"There is one student who makes me so mad. I dread it every day."

"I know I snap sometimes. I hate myself for it after."

"I try so hard to connect with them. They just don't seem to respond."

Teachers are not failing because they do not care. They are failing because nobody ever taught them what is actually happening in their classroom. The hormones, the home life, the unprocessed trauma, the identity crises — all of it walks through the door every morning. And the teacher catches it all.

This programme gives teachers what they were never given: a map of the emotional world of the young people in front of them. And crucially — it helps them understand their own triggers, their own reactions, and how to regulate themselves first.

A teacher who understands themselves changes the room. A teacher who understands their students changes lives. The EmpowerED Teacher is the only CPD programme in the UK that tackles this directly.

The Girl Code Compass
She already knows. She just needs to remember.
Ages 11–18
Most suitable for: Secondary schools · Girls pastoral groups · SEND girls · Youth clubs · Faith groups · Community orgs
✦ View the session guide →

Designed to support girls aged 11 to 18 in navigating their inner world — their feelings, thoughts, identity and sense of worth — at one of the most complex stages of their lives.

In a safe, closed circle, girls are gently guided to understand the emotions beneath their behaviour: the shame that hides behind anger, the overwhelm that looks like not caring, the longing to be seen that can pull them towards connections they are not yet ready for.

This programme helps young women discover who they are beneath all the noise — so that when the world tries to define them, they already know themselves. So that they know their own worth before anyone else tries to give it to them.

Six sessions. One circle. A compass for life.

How it works
Ready to bring EmpowerED to your organisation?

There is no basket or automated checkout for our bespoke programmes. Every booking is personal. Sofia reviews every enquiry individually and sends a tailored proposal and invoice within 48 hours. No obligation.

1. Enquire
Click any enquire button or email Sofia directly. Tell her your organisation, what you are interested in and your preferred dates.
2. Proposal
Sofia sends a tailored proposal within 48 hours — programme, dates, pricing and any adaptations for your organisation.
3. Invoice & payment
Sofia sends a QuickBooks invoice with a secure Stripe payment link. Pay by card online instantly. BACS also accepted. Payment plans available on request.
4. Confirmed
Your programme is booked. Sofia handles all preparation, materials and facilitator coordination from here.
