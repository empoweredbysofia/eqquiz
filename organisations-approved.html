Flagship · Girls · Ages 11–18 · Group Programme
The Girl Code Compass
She already knows. She just needs to remember.
Ages 11–18
Most suitable for: Secondary schools · Girls pastoral groups · SEND girls · Youth clubs · Faith groups · Community orgs
✦ View the session guide →

Designed to support girls in navigating their inner world, their feelings, thoughts, identity and sense of worth, at one of the most complex and formative stages of their lives. In a safe, closed circle, girls are gently guided to understand the emotions beneath their behaviour: the shame that hides behind anger, the overwhelm that shows up as withdrawal, the longing to be seen that can pull them towards connections they are not yet ready for. This programme helps young women discover who they are beneath all the noise, so that when the world tries to define them, they already know themselves. Six sessions. One circle. A compass for life.
