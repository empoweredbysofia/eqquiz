1:1 Coaching with Sofia

This is for women who are ready.

Not ready to feel better. Ready to understand why they feel the way they do — and to change it, permanently.

This is not for everyone.

Sofia works with a small number of women at a time. The work is intensive, personal, and built entirely around your EQ Audit results and where you are in your journey.

If you are looking for someone to tell you it will all be okay, this is not that. If you are ready for the honest truth about your emotional patterns — and the tools to change them — keep reading.
