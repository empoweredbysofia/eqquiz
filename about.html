The Woman Behind the Work

Sofia did not set out to become an emotional intelligence coach. She set out to survive.

Her Story

For years, Sofia was the woman who felt everything and understood none of it. She cried in meetings. She took everything personally. She kept repeating the same patterns in her relationships and her business — and she could not understand why.

Nobody had ever taught her what to do with her emotions. Not her family. Not school. Not the personal development books she devoured. They told her to think positive. To be grateful. To push through. None of it touched the real problem.

"The problem was not my emotions. It was that I had never been given the tools to understand them. Once I had those tools, everything changed."

The change came when Sofia discovered the science of emotional intelligence — not as a corporate buzzword, but as a real, learnable set of skills. She trained. She studied. She applied every framework to her own life before she ever brought it to a client.

What she found was that emotional intelligence was not about becoming calmer or less reactive. It was about understanding what your emotions are telling you, and knowing what to do next. That distinction changed her life.

Her Work

Today Sofia works with women who are at every stage of that journey — from the woman who has just started to notice something feels wrong, to the woman who is close to her breakthrough and needs someone to guide the final stretch.

She also brings that same work into schools, businesses, and organisations — because emotional intelligence is not a personal problem. It is a human one. And it can be taught.

Her approach is direct and warm. She does not use clinical language or self-help clichés. She speaks to the woman who is tired of being told to just breathe through it — and she shows her what else is possible.

The best place to begin is with honest self-knowledge.
