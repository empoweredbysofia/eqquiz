Who we are

Spark & Spectrum is a Community Interest Company founded by Sofia Iqbal and Selina Afzal. We create neurodiversity-affirming programmes that help neurodivergent individuals, their families and the people who support them understand, explore and celebrate the unique ways they think, process and experience the world.

We work in schools, community settings and organisations — alongside EmpowerED by Sofia's wider SEND offer — to provide a joined-up, human-first approach to neurodivergent support.

Our programmes are not diagnostic tools. They are spaces for gentle, reflective exploration — affirming, accessible and built around lived experience.

Our philosophy
🧬
Neurodivergence is natural
Not a deficit, not a disorder. A natural variation in human neurology that brings its own strengths, perceptions and ways of being.
💛
Inherent value in every person
Every individual has worth, gifts and a unique configuration of traits. Our work starts from that truth, not from what needs fixing.
🪞
Self-knowledge is the foundation
Understanding yourself — how you think, feel and process — is the first step to wellbeing, confidence and connection.
🌿
Gentle exploration over assessment
We use reflective prompts, not clinical questions. Our spaces are non-pressured, affirming and designed to make space for every kind of response.
Our approach
🎯 Reflective, not diagnostic
Prompts that invite curiosity rather than clinical categories. We centre lived experience over external assessment.
🕰️ Self-paced
No time pressure. Participants engage at their own pace with space for written, visual or contemplative responses.
🗣️ Affirming language
Non-clinical, accessible language that validates without toxic positivity. We recognise systemic barriers and masking.
🔀 Non-linear engagement
Materials can be engaged with in any order. Options for participation include writing, drawing, talking or simply thinking.
🤝 Agency & self-determination
We emphasise each person's right to understand and define their own experience — on their terms.
🌐 Intersectionality recognised
We make space for individual variation, cultural context and the ways identity, race and neurodivergence intersect.

Understanding Your Spark

Self-discovery

The Spark is your unique configuration of traits, passions, perceptions and ways of being. This programme invites neurodivergent individuals to explore their cognitive patterns, sensory experiences, strengths and challenges — not through clinical assessment but through gentle, affirming self-inquiry.

Delivered in schools, community groups, therapeutic contexts and peer support settings. Available as a workshop series, self-paced resource or facilitated programme.

Who we support — the spectrum

Spark & Spectrum recognises neurodivergence as a spectrum that includes — but is not limited to — the following:

Autism (ASC / ASD)
ADHD / ADD
Dyslexia
Dyspraxia / DCD
Dyscalculia
Tourette Syndrome
OCD & anxiety (co-occurring)
Sensory processing differences

This is not a diagnostic service. Our programmes complement but do not replace professional support.
