Session One
This Is a Safe Circle
Who are we, really?: underneath the performance, the mask, the noise

The first session is about creating something most girls have never experienced: a space where they do not have to perform. No right answers. No judgement. No need to be anything other than exactly who they are right now.


We establish the circle: its agreements, its boundaries and its promise. And then, gently, we begin. Girls introduce not who the world thinks they are, but who they actually are. This is often the first time they have been asked that question without an agenda.


Each girl receives her Girl Code Compass journal, her companion for all six sessions and beyond.

What we explore
Circle agreements: what we say here, stays here
Who am I really?: beneath the labels and the likes
Introduction to values: the beginning of the code
✦ Journal: My first page: who I am right now

Session Two
My Story, My Feelings
Not my shame. Understanding the emotions beneath the surface

This is the session where girls are given permission: perhaps for the first time: to tell the truth about their inner world. Family breakdown. Absent fathers. Feeling invisible at home. Carrying too much, too young. Being the strong one when they needed someone to be strong for them.


We hold all of it with gentleness and complete respect. We help girls understand that the anger, the withdrawal, the numbness: these are not character flaws. They are emotions in disguise. Shame that looks like attitude. Grief that looks like not caring. Overwhelm that looks like rebellion.


What happened to you is not your identity. But understanding it is where freedom begins.

What we explore
My emotional history, mapping feelings to moments
Emotions in disguise, anger, shame, grief & overwhelm
Emotional vocabulary, naming what lives inside us
✦ Journal: A letter to the younger me (optional, private)

Session Three
My Code
The values I live by. Discovering what is truly non-negotiable

A girl who knows her code is unshakeable. She does not need to borrow her identity from the group. She does not need approval to know her own worth. She does not compromise what matters to her because someone else wants her to.


In this session, each girl creates her personal Girl Code: the values that are hers alone. Not what her parents want. Not what her friends expect. Not what social media tells her she should be. Her actual code. The one that lives in her chest when she is quiet enough to listen.


This is the antidote to peer pressure. To the wrong friendship. To shrinking to fit somewhere she was never meant to stay.

What we explore
Values deep dive, what truly matters to me
My non-negotiables, what I will and will not accept
When I betrayed my code, and what it cost me

Session Four
Relationships
What I deserve vs what I am settling for: knowing worth before seeking love

This is the session held with the greatest gentleness: and the greatest honesty. We explore what girls are really looking for when they seek closeness: to be seen, to feel chosen, to not be alone, to feel worthy. These are real and beautiful needs.


The conversation touches on the pull towards early romantic relationships: not to lecture, not to shame, but to help girls understand what they are truly looking for and whether that place can actually give it to them. A girl who knows her worth does not settle. A girl who understands her code does not give herself to someone who cannot honour it.


We speak about the difference between choosing from fullness and clinging from emptiness: gently, honestly, without judgement.

What we explore
What am I really looking for?: beneath the attraction
Healthy connection vs seeking validation: the difference
Boundaries from worth: not from rules or fear

Session Five
My Reset
Tools for the hard days. A toolkit that is built for exactly who she is

By now, girls know more about themselves than when they walked into the first session. They know their values. They understand their emotions. They are beginning to see how their story shaped them without defining them.


This session gives them the practical tools for when it gets hard: because it will get hard. The overwhelm will come. The family tension will flare. The friendship will hurt. The situation they thought they had handled will surface again.


Sofia's emotional reset method is adapted here specifically for girls: not a generic breathing exercise, but a real toolkit each girl builds for herself. What works for her body, her mind, her life. What she reaches for first. Who she calls. What she does when she needs to come back to herself.

What we explore
The reset method, personalised for her
My safe people, who I reach for and why
Regulation strategies that fit her life, not a textbook

Session Six
She Knows
The Girl Code Compass ceremony: she takes her compass home

The final session is not a lesson. It is a celebration. Six weeks ago, these girls walked into a room as strangers. They leave as a circle: with each other's stories held carefully, their own stories honoured, and something solid inside them that was not there before.


Each girl shares one thing she knows about herself that she did not know six weeks ago. Then she receives her Girl Code Compass: a beautifully designed, physical take-home that holds her values, her code, her reset toolkit and her commitments to herself.


This session is designed to move girls. Not to break them: to open them. To remind them of what they came here to find. And to send them back into the world with a compass that belongs only to them.

What we celebrate
One thing I know now that I didn't: shared in the circle
Circle closing ritual: what this space gave us
