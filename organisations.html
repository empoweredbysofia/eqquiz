For Organisations

Emotional intelligence is not a soft skill. It is the skill.

Sofia works with schools, businesses, and community organisations to build emotionally intelligent cultures from the inside out.

Why emotional intelligence changes everything.

Stress, burnout, conflict, and disengagement are not management problems. They are emotional intelligence problems. When your people understand their own emotions, they communicate better, lead better, and perform better.

Sofia brings the same honest, grounded approach she uses with individual clients into organisational settings — without the corporate gloss or the self-help clichés.

"The most emotionally intelligent organisations are not the ones that eliminate difficult feelings. They are the ones where people have been taught what to do with them."

— Sofia
What Sofia Offers

Programmes designed around your people.

Every session is tailored to your organisation, your staff, and the specific challenges you are navigating.

I

Workshops

Half-day and full-day workshops on emotional regulation, self-awareness, and interpersonal communication. Practical, evidence-informed, and designed for real working environments.

II

Keynote Speaking

Sofia speaks at events, conferences, and staff development days on emotional intelligence, women's wellbeing, and the neuroscience of emotion. Compelling, personal, and always honest.

III

Staff Wellbeing Sessions

Ongoing wellbeing support for your team — whether monthly drop-ins, structured programmes, or crisis-informed training. Sofia meets your staff where they are.

IV

Training Programmes

Multi-week programmes that build emotional intelligence skills across teams. Designed for sustained change, not a one-off session. Includes pre and post EQ assessments.

V

Student Wellbeing — Years 6 to 13

Age-appropriate emotional intelligence sessions for students at critical transition points. Sofia works directly with young people using the same honest, non-patronising approach she is known for.
