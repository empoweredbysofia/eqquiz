Who we are

A community built on lived experience

Spark & Spectrum is a Community Interest Company founded by Selina Afzal and Sofia Iqbal. We create programmes for neurodivergent individuals, those with special and medical needs including vision loss, their families and the people who support them.

We work in schools, community settings and organisations to deliver a human-first approach grounded in lived experience, compassionate practice and affirming exploration.

Our programmes are not diagnostic tools. They are spaces for gentle, reflective exploration — affirming, accessible and built around the whole person.

Our philosophy

What we believe

Difference is natural

Not a deficit. A natural variation in neurology and physiology that brings its own strengths, perceptions and ways of being.

Inherent value in every person

Every individual has worth, gifts and a unique configuration of traits. Our work starts from that truth. Not from what needs fixing.

Self-knowledge is the foundation

Understanding yourself, how you think, feel and process, is the first step to wellbeing, confidence and connection.

Gentle exploration over assessment

Reflective prompts, not clinical questions. Non-pressured spaces for every kind of response.

Inclusive of medical needs

We extend beyond neurodivergence to include vision loss, complex medical needs and acquired conditions, with the same affirming, human-first lens.

Community before clinic

We go where people are. Delivered in schools, community spaces, faith settings and families — accessible, not gatekept.

Dyspraxia / DCD

Vision loss
