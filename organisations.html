Psychology & modalities

Grounded in evidence, delivered with humanity

Our programmes draw from a blend of established psychological frameworks and therapeutic modalities, thoughtfully woven together, never used in isolation, always person-first.

Foundation
Emotional Intelligence (EQ)
The core of everything we do, recognising, understanding and managing emotions in self and others. Based on the Salovey–Mayer and Goleman models.
Framework
Cognitive Behavioural Approaches
Identifying thought patterns, challenging unhelpful beliefs and building healthier responses. Used within resilience and rewire programmes.
Regulation
Polyvagal & Nervous System Informed
Understanding the body's stress responses and how to regulate from the inside out. Central to co-regulation, SEND and parenting programmes.
Trauma-Informed
Trauma-Informed Practice
Safety, trust and choice at the centre of every session. Recognising how adverse experiences shape behaviour, without retraumatising.
Identity
Strengths-Based & Narrative Approaches
Rewriting limiting stories, finding the person beneath the diagnosis or behaviour. Used throughout neurodiversity and empowerment programmes.
Neurodiversity
Double Empathy & Affirmative Practice
Replacing deficit-based models with identity-affirming frameworks. Rooted in the social model of disability and lived-experience research.
Mindfulness
Mindfulness & Somatic Awareness
Body-based techniques that build present-moment awareness and reduce reactivity. Adapted for sensory differences and neurodivergent participants.
Positive Psychology
Values, Meaning & Flourishing
Drawing from Seligman's PERMA model, building wellbeing from what's working, not only from what's wrong. Used in student and workplace programmes.
Attachment
Attachment Theory & Relational Safety
Understanding how early relational experiences shape behaviour in school, home and work. Especially relevant in The EmpowerED Teacher and parenting programmes.

Our facilitators do not apply these as rigid clinical protocols. They are lenses, held lightly, combined thoughtfully, and always led by what the person in the room needs.
