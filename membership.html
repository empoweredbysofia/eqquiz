Why a Membership

Transformation is not a one-off event.

The EQ Audit shows you where you are. The ebook gives you the foundations. But real change happens over time — in the moments between sessions, in the conversations that challenge you, in the weeks when everything feels harder than it should.

The Reset Room exists for those weeks. A community of women at every EQ stage, led by Sofia, doing the same work at the same time.

"Emotional intelligence is a practice, not a destination. The Reset Room is where the practice happens."

— Sofia
What's Included

Everything you need to keep moving forward.

Your membership gives you access to all of the following, every month.

◆

Monthly Live Session with Sofia

A live group session each month focused on a specific aspect of emotional intelligence. Ask questions, go deeper, and hear Sofia work through the material in real time.

◆

Monthly Reset Workshop

A practical, recorded workshop released each month — with tools, exercises, and frameworks you can apply immediately. Organised by EQ stage.

◆

Private Community Access

A private space for Reset Room members only. Share, ask, support, and be supported by women at every stage of the EQ journey. Sofia is active in the community every week.

◆

The Stage Library

A growing library of resources organised by EQ stage — so wherever your Audit placed you, you have exactly the right material to work with right now.

◆

Monthly Check-In & Progress Tracker

A structured monthly reflection to track your movement through the EQ stages. See how far you have come and what to focus on next.
